Privacy & terms

Channel Queue is an independent CoolCode research workspace using YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Public lookups send the supplied IDs, handles or search terms to the protected CoolCode gateway and YouTube. We do not request YouTube login credentials, account permissions, private subscriptions or private playlists.

YouTube metadata is held in tab memory only and expires within 30 days. Your deliberately saved bookmarks, labels, topics, notes and manual resume positions use browser storage on this device. They are not uploaded by this app. “Clear workspace” removes that local data immediately; it does not delete anything from YouTube. Exported backup files remain wherever you save them and must be deleted separately.

Thumbnails contact YouTube image hosts. The official player, loaded only on request, may show advertisements, collect playback/device information and use cookies or similar technologies under the Google Privacy Policy. No autoplay is requested. Where child-directed status is true or cannot be verified, the app offers a YouTube link instead of embedding. The app does not download videos or audio. CoolCode separately counts aggregate tool opens; notes and queries are excluded. For privacy questions, contact the maintainer.
